## Brand & Style

The design system frames holistic health and nutritional awareness as a refined daily ritual rather than an analytical chore. Tailored for conscious young adults and wellness-forward professionals, the aesthetic balances the restorative tranquility of deep botanicals with the crisp structure of modern editorial publications. 

The visual personality combines quiet confidence with warm approachability. By blending a serene organic palette with modern geometric typography and controlled atmospheric layering, the interface creates an emotional sensation of clarity, vitality, and grounding. Layouts favor generous breathing room, intentional alignment, and tailored content hierarchies that invite deliberate engagement over sensory overload.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical and earth-derived tones that mirror raw ingredients and organic rituals.

- **Primary (`#2A5A40`)**: A grounding, deep forest sage used for signature branding moments, primary calls-to-action, structural navigation headers, and primary active states.
- **Secondary (`#E07A5F`)**: An active terracotta coral that injects focused vitality. It highlights key nutritional milestones, streaks, active energy counters, and timely prompts without disrupting the overarching calm.
- **Tertiary (`#6B8F71`)**: A soft lichen green used for secondary indicators, supporting tags, positive nutritional markers, and peaceful accents.
- **Neutral Base & Charcoal (`#1F2421`)**: Primary textual elements leverage a rich slate charcoal rather than stark pitch black, anchoring copy with warm, ink-like contrast. Background surfaces rely on layered warm creams and oat tones (`#FBF7F0` canvas, `#FFFDF9` surface layers) to preserve organic warmth.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites geometric poise with structural readability.

- **Headlines (`Outfit`)**: Clean, circular letterforms provide an architectural silhouette. Display and headline levels establish rhythm across nutrition digests and ritual summaries, using negative tracking at larger scales to maintain compact cohesion.
- **Body & Labels (`Manrope`)**: Highly legible humanist construction delivers optimal legibility for nutritional figures, meal ingredients, and educational content. Medium and bold weights give small metadata crisp delineation on high-density mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an 8-point baseline grid designed to support responsive fluidity across handheld screens and wider viewing environments:

- **Mobile (< 600px)**: A 4-column fluid layout with a base outer margin of `1.25rem` and gutters of `1rem`. Core content modules prioritize vertical stacking with rapid horizontal carousels for meal and micro-habit cards.
- **Tablet (600px – 1024px)**: An 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content segments split into dual-column arrangements (e.g., daily tracking alongside nutritional breakdowns).
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: A 12-column layout centered within a max-width container of `1200px`, utilizing `3rem` canvas margins and `2rem` gutters.

Component internals adhere strictly to standard rhythm tokens: `space-xs` (4px) for inline icon offsets, `space-sm` (8px) for badge and chip insets, `space-md` (16px) for card body padding, and `space-lg` (24px) for card section separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy drop shadows, relying instead on ambient light, tonal stratification, and frosted translucent overlays:

- **Surface Base**: Background canvas sits on warm wheat cream (`#FBF7F0`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards)**: Opaque soft cream (`#FFFDF9`) paired with a micro-border of `rgba(42, 90, 64, 0.08)`. Subtle tinting prevents cold starkness.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating & Interactive Panels)**: Translucent white surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) supported by a `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a delicate ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.06)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Navigation)**: Elevated translucent panels (`rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.92)`) with a `16px` backdrop blur, a fine inner outline of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, and a diffused ground shadow: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a calibrated rounded geometry (Scale 2), balancing organic softness with editorial precision:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem`)**: Applied to compact controls, including input fields, checkboxes, dropdown menus, and utility buttons.
- **Large Radius (`1rem`)**: Applied to content cards, modal sheets, insight cards, and meal overview modules.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`1.5rem`)**: Applied to hero panels, floating dynamic sheets, and banner containers.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for functional status chips, active metric badges, and primary action triggers to retain an approachable feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid forest sage background (`#2A5A40`), stark white label text (`#FFFFFF`), standard radius `0.5rem` or full pill radius for floating quick-actions. Hover/active states darken subtly to `#204531`.
- **Secondary / Vitality**: Terracotta coral background (`#E07A5F`), white label text, designed for active motivational prompts, habit check-ins, and streak loggers.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent container, `1.5px` border in `rgba(42, 90, 64, 0.25)`, forest sage label text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Nutritional / Macro Tags**: Pill-shaped containers (`9999px`) featuring tinted background fills (`rgba(42, 90, 64, 0.08)` or `rgba(224, 122, 95, 0.1)`), paired with typography set at `label-md` in matching tonal ink.
- **Interactive Filter Chips**: Semi-transparent cream fill with a hairline border (`rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.12)`). Selected state shifts to solid `#2A5A40` with white text.

### Cards
- **Meal & Recipe Modules**: Layered cream background (`#FFFDF9`), `1rem` corner radius, `space-md` interior padding, and a micro-border in `rgba(42, 90, 64, 0.06)`. Header imagery adopts the same `1rem` top radius.
- **Glassmorphic Metric Cards**: Translucent frosted panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) with `12px` blur, highlighting active numbers (e.g., hydration levels, micronutrient percentages) in `Outfit` headlines.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp ivory container (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` radius, `1px` border in `rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.15)`. Focus triggers a `1.5px` outer boundary in forest sage (`#2A5A40`) with a soft matching glow (`rgba(42, 90, 64, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: `0.375rem` radius for checkboxes; circular for radios. Inactive border is `rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.25)`. Checked state transitions to `#2A5A40` with a sharp white checkmark glyph.

### Habit Ring & Progress Indicators
- **Circadian & Macro Bars**: Multi-tier linear and circular indicators with soft organic track backgrounds (`rgba(42, 90, 64, 0.1)`) and progress strokes in primary forest sage (`#2A5A40`) or vitality terracotta (`#E07A5F`), rounded on terminal caps.
